# "Study Table" prototype: https://claude.ai/artifact/UCUDsFj9YRPgMPmC7PrbZ9#table-sfmj

# About
A quiet online study table for friends: set your goals, focus together, and check in every hour to show your work.

# Testing

## User 1, Nick

**Copy is confusing, especially at check-in.** The wording didn't make sense to them, and the check-in screen didn't feel helpful.

**Chat or call during check-in?** They asked whether check-in should be a typed chat or a quick call.

**Proof of work.** They suggested sending a photo or screenshot of your work at check-in. This raises a bigger question: how do people give each other real feedback, not just reactions?

**Not everyone has study friends.** Not everyone has friends ready to study with. They suggested public rooms or servers you could join.

**What I'll change:**
- Rewrite the check-in copy so it's clearer
- Add photo/screenshot uploads to check-ins
- Look into public rooms for people studying alone

## User 2, My dad

**What he did:**
- Added 3 goals right away and liked pressing Enter to add each one.
- Didn't notice the friends around the table at first. 
- Pressed Pause by accident while looking for a way to leave.

**What I'll change:**
- Label the circles with names, not just initials
- Add a short prompt in "show your work", like "What did you finish?"
- Move "Leave" somewhere easier to find
