# 11/14 | Live version test notes

I tested at the library with 5 people, all with their laptops with the new live version.

## What works

- **Joining:** each person types a name, and the bar in the corner shows **Live · 5 here** with a circle for each person.
- **Adding an idea:** Ana added "Picnic at the lake," and it showed up on the others' open bucket list 1–2 seconds later, marked "added by A."
- **Editing a page:** When multiple added a sticker, everyone saw it. When someone dragged it, users saw it jump to the new spot when they let go.

## Problems

1. **Everyone's circle in the "who's here" bar is the same peach color**, so people are hard to tell apart.
2. **Other people see items jump, not slide.** Moves show up when the item is dropped, not while it's being dragged.
