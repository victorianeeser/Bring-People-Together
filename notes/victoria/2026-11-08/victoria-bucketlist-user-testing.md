# 11/8 | Bucket List user testing

Testing the [Bucket List prototype](bucketlist/README.md) with 5 people. Under each tester, **Prototype response** shows what changed because of what they said.

## What I want to find out
- Do people understand it's a shared list for a friend group?
- Can they add a plan, join one, and finish one without help?
- Does turning a finished plan into a scrapbook page feel worth it?
- Would this actually get a group to do the things on the list?

---

## How to run each test
**Before each person:**
- Go to Settings and click **Reset demo data**
- Have 2 photos saved on the laptop for the scrapbook task

**Say:** "This is a rough prototype of a shared bucket list. You're Hannah, and these are your roommates. I'm testing the app, not you, so please think out loud."

**First impression:** Show the Bucket List page and ask, "What do you think this is?"

**Tasks** (don't help unless they're stuck):
1. Add something you want to do with your friends this winter.
2. Show only the plans for spring.
3. You want to go to the living room movie marathon. Let your friends know.
4. Your group finished painting pottery. Mark it done and make a scrapbook page with a photo, some text, and a sticker. Turn the sticker a little.
5. Find your page in the scrapbook, then flip through the book.

**Ask after:**
1. What was confusing or harder than you expected?
2. Which group of yours would you use this with, if any?
3. What would make you open it again after adding your plans?
4. Would making a scrapbook page make you more likely to do the plans? Why or why not?
5. What's missing?
